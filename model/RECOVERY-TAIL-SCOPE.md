# Natural-season recovery tail

This is a separate observation-only final-run controller. It is not a new-save
policy, a finite-expected-time theorem, or an optimal policy. It does not change
the frozen new-save experiment or the default completion controller.

The host must commit to maintenance at 86,400,000 ms and every 86,400,000 ms
thereafter **before the new save is created**. `naturalRecoveryTailPlan` records
this schedule and an unreleased initial memory. A prefix may release the tail
only after its ordinary progression work. The tail cannot request a reload or
choose the schedule from an outcome.

The entry check requires the following public conditions:

- An active normal run with at least 100 ascensions, all heavenly upgrades,
  dragon level 27, Cursor level 12, Farm level 10, and the other minigame levels.
- Every required achievement outside the tail's named collection, production
  and aggregate ownership families, plus Hardcore, Neverclick, A wizard is you, Thick-skinned, Eldeer,
  Wrath cookie, Early bird, Fading luck, Black cat's paw, and the Santa helper.
- All finite toggle access except the Covenant/pledge and Golden switch controls
  that the tail uses itself; no live paid-season timer; an office no higher than 4.
- Loaded Garden and market, no unrelated entry prompt, auras 0, finite resource
  values, at most 2000 of each building, levels no higher than 12, and no debug
  upgrades. The observed bank/counter/CpS bounds are checked, not inferred from
  the size of the fixture.

The entry is stricter than the current completion controller. In particular,
that controller normally postpones Farm level 10 until its final milk check.
The separate ordered candidate now supplies an explicit Farm 10 gate and a
planned handoff. No complete new-save trajectory or properness proof is supplied.
An entry error is explicit; the tail does not reset to conceal it. The source
partition retains 39 aggregate current-ownership awards in the tail, including
the 700-upgrade award; their purchases cannot be required before this collection.

After the entry, the controller does the following:

- It drains existing buffs. Sugar blessing and Gifted out are exempt because
  they do not multiply production or click income. It does not create new
  golden-cookie, spell, loan, aura, or Sugar frenzy buffs.
- It leaves every golden cookie untouched, including Fortune-created cookies.
  Christmas uses native reindeer. Halloween and Easter use wrinkler payouts.
- It uses whichever needed natural season is currently active. It never buys a
  season trigger, changes the clock, or resets to obtain a season or outcome.
  Missing Valentine cookies do not block an active Christmas window.
- It uses dirt throughout Garden collection, one sacrifice and the rebuilt seed
  log, the seven drops, and the harvest/plot achievements. It does not refill a
  minigame or spend lumps on repeated attempts.
- It uses fixed visible-price market thresholds. Holdings and each transaction
  are limited to 1000 shares. Office 4 remains in place until seasonal, Garden,
  Hand-picked and the seven non-office market achievements are recorded. The
  native office 5 action then supplies its current unlock and Pyramid scheme.
- It reserves the next quoted seasonal/Garden resource request before market
  trades and production purchases. Production buys only its finite count goals
  and ordinary upgrades; there is no unbounded baseline building investment.
- It confirms only a prompt it requested at the same elapsed time and menu.
  The scheduled maintenance Options/language prompt gets no such authority.
  Missing pre-restoration progress causes a wait with existing memory retained.
- It calls the unchanged full `assessCompletion` predicate before returning
  `complete`. This includes current ownership, all finite access, 600 normal
  flags, and the separate native achievement counter.

The controller's guards express candidate invariants. They are not proofs that
all native callbacks preserve them. A violation stops at an explicit boundary.
The following work is still required for a proper full-game route:

1. Construct and prove the legal prefix from a fresh save, including the entry
   bank, finite board, the required lump levels, all reset work, and the counted
   golden-cookie phase. A synthetic entry fixture does not supply this prefix.
2. Prove resource recovery and absence of starvation after every failed Garden,
   wrinkler, pet, fortune and market attempt. The scalar earning barriers and
   catalogue bounds do not by themselves prove affordable replanting or trade
   entry at every reachable callback state.
3. Prove the native random-generator phase and a uniform conditional success
   event for the actual interleaved retries. Existing fixed-tape cylinders and
   finite seeded diagnostics do not prove this composition.
4. Compose the calendar recurrence with actual daily request, save, launch and
   restoration completion. The native 100 ms load gate is a lower bound, not an
   upper bound on readiness. The current mathematical calendar is explicitly
   UTC; local-time and DST correspondence for another environment is not proved.
5. Refine physical inputs, source callbacks, save quantization, notifications,
   and first-hit monitoring. The Node diagnostic action adapter is not a proof
   of the complete native-input kernel. No matching lower bound or attainment
   result follows from these controller tests.

Tests use explicit conditional snapshots and funded native-source fixtures.
They distinguish observed controller decisions from intact native actions.
The Santa-to-office suffix reaches the full predicate from its supplied entry;
it is not a completing trajectory from a new save. All original season, market,
and Garden controller tests are also run with their default options.
